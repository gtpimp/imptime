Long term support

Software has a lifetime and needs to be maintained, for example:
  • the systems it connects to change and the connections need to be fixed
  • the styles of the times change and the solution needs to be kept fresh and relevant
  • new requirements and ideas arise which need to be incorporated into the solution
  • features which haven't been used for months start to become important and need extra work
  • hardware fails
ImplicitDesign values its client base and we grow with our clients. We treat every project as a long term project. Our project process is flexible enough to manage the initial intensive development, and the extended support for that system, however long or short. Part of a stress-free development process is knowing the life of the project is assured. Where your final solution is an in-house development team, or whether you continue to use our services for the life-time of your product, we will tailor the sprint management to suit you.
